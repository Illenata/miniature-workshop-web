# Terms

*Draft. The game team is preparing the final text.*

This site shows miniatures painted in the game Miniature Workshop. A miniature can only be uploaded from the game, with the "Share" button.

## What you may publish

Share miniatures you painted yourself. You may not publish:

- offensive content: painted on the miniature, or in its title, description or workshop name;
- spam and advertising;
- other people's work under your name.

## Everything you share is public

A miniature appears in the gallery and on your workshop page, and anyone with the link can open it. Its 3D model and card can be downloaded.

## Moderation

Anyone can report a miniature with the button on its page. A miniature reported by several people is hidden until a moderator reviews it. The moderator may hide or delete a miniature and block a workshop that breaks these rules. A blocked workshop cannot upload new miniatures.

## Deletion at the owner's request

You can ask for your miniature or your whole workshop to be deleted: see the [Privacy](/privacy) page.

## Changes

These terms may change. The current version is always on this page.
